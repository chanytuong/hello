## Verdict meaning and counting

- **PASS:** no identified major visible construction or gameplay-fit flaw within the stated evidence limits. This would approve a principle for consideration, never a source frame for copying.
- **FIX:** useful concepts/contrasts remain, with specific failures to resolve in an entirely original construction. It does not preserve this sequence, pose or raster.
- **REPLACE:** reject the sequence/study as a motion blueprint because its core action, continuity, construction or game fit is unsuitable. A general visual principle may still be noted.

Counts refer to visible samples, not production frame counts, exposures or timing. There are 75 motion/effect samples and 10 static samples. DEATH has four body poses plus one ember-only sample. EXTRA/EFFECT has six different sketches, not six confirmed sequential frames. Head studies may also be alternatives. Palette swatches are discussed as style evidence, not an animation group.

Left-to-right numbering below identifies positions on the board; it does not certify temporal order. All evidence about center of gravity, pinning and arcs is visual inference. Montage positions are not registered root coordinates or physics trajectories. Unknown timing is not invented as a defect; each verdict rests on the visible issues and the actual game's constraints.
