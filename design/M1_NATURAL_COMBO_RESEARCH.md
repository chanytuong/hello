## Current candidate diagnosis

Read-only opening of the actual 21-Action file confirms 101 bones and the exact saved camera. [Audit](../artifacts/m1_natural_combo/CURRENT_NATIVE_AUDIT.json). At first contact the wrist is 0.250 / 0.290 / 0.321 m below its shoulder, with forward-vector pitch approximately −28° / −39° / −48°. The downward aim is authored, not a playback rounding problem. The first two no-chain exits last only **0.0667 s** after their handoff. Their final whole-body blend also blends supporting legs while both feet are catching up. Exact chain-channel equality proved continuity at a point, but did not establish a natural standalone recovery.

The new design raises all attack paths to chest/shoulder height, keeps hip compression small, and gives each no-chain exit a longer compact balance continuation with one catching foot and one supporting foot. At the chain decision, either continue directly from the shared recoil or commit to that stop continuation. A late press must not jump backward from stop recovery into an old chain pose. Actual enemy dimensions remain untouched; use separately named player-height diagnostic dummies and document the old scale mismatch. Research is a prerequisite; native/gameplay results belong to the design and QA deliverables.
